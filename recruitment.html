Join Us

CHENJ-Lab warmly welcomes highly motivated students and researchers who are interested in developmental biology, embryonic development, congenital defects, and related mechanisms of disease. We are committed to building a creative, collaborative, and supportive research environment. If you are enthusiastic about scientific discovery and would like to work on important questions in development and disease, we would be delighted to hear from you.

Open Positions

Postdoctoral Fellows

We welcome applicants with a Ph.D. in developmental biology, cell biology, genetics, molecular biology, bioinformatics, or related fields. Candidates with strong publication records, independent thinking, and enthusiasm for interdisciplinary research are especially encouraged to apply. Applicants for postdoctoral positions are also encouraged to include a list of publications and the contact information of referees.

Graduate Students

We welcome graduate students who are interested in embryonic development, developmental disorders, and disease-related mechanisms. Students with backgrounds in biology, medicine, genetics, bioinformatics, or related areas are encouraged to contact us.

Undergraduate Students

Motivated undergraduate students who are interested in scientific research are welcome to join the lab for training, research projects, and academic development.

We Are Looking For

  • Strong interest in developmental biology and biomedical research
  • Curiosity, responsibility, and enthusiasm for scientific exploration
  • Good communication and teamwork skills
  • Willingness to learn new techniques and think independently

What We Offer

  • A supportive and collaborative research environment
  • Training in advanced experimental techniques and scientific writing
  • Opportunities for academic growth and career development
  • Access to exciting research questions in development and disease

How to Apply

Please send your CV, a brief statement of research interests, and relevant supporting materials to: jingchen(at)scu.edu.cn
